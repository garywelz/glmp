# Mathematics — 50-Process Scheme (2025-01, superseded)

**Provenance:** Extracted from `copernicus-web/huggingface-space/DISCIPLINE_DATABASES_PLAN.md`,
Section 4 ("Mathematics Database"), originally drafted January 2025 as one of five
planned discipline flowchart databases modeled after GLMP.

**Status: superseded.** This 50-process, 5-subcategory scheme was never implemented.
Math/ATAP instead grew organically as a corpus of proof graphs and axiomatic-theory
dependency graphs (`garywelz/atap`), not as a fixed-count discipline collection
following this plan. Archived here as the early development record of the
Programming Framework method's first mathematics-application design — not a live
spec, not a to-do list.
