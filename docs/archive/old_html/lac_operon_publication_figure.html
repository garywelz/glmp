Figure 1: Lac Operon Regulation as a Computational Program. Environmental inputs (green) include lactose availability, glucose levels, and energy status. The system processes these through 4 OR gates (yellow diamonds) for flexible sensing, 2 AND gates (purple hexagons) for signal integration, and 2 NOT gates (red trapezoids) for repression control. Processing steps (blue) lead to enzyme production (orange) and lactose metabolism (black circle). Dashed lines show positive feedback loops. This 29-node representation demonstrates Boolean logic in gene regulation.
