Photosynthesis: Nature's Solar Energy System

Photosynthesis represents the most sophisticated energy conversion system in biology, converting light energy into chemical energy through precisely coordinated electron transport, proton pumping, and carbon fixation reactions. This system demonstrates how biological organisms implement energy harvesting, signal transduction, and metabolic integration at the molecular level.
