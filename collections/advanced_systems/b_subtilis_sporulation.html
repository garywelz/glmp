B. subtilis Sporulation: Bacterial Developmental Programming

Bacillus subtilis sporulation represents one of the most sophisticated examples of bacterial developmental programming, featuring asymmetric cell division, sigma factor cascades, and compartment-specific gene expression. This system demonstrates how bacterial cells implement complex developmental logic through temporal and spatial regulation of gene expression.
